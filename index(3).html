📖 How to Use Lost & Fessy-ound

Home
View the newest lost/found posts, search for items, and use the Lost Items / Found Items filters.
Submit
Post something you lost or found. Add the item name, description, type, hashtag, contact information, and optionally a photo.
Account Info
Shows your current username, email, role, and account approval status.
Lost Items / Found Items
Quickly filter the Home page. Click the same filter again to return to all items.
Email / Phone
Contact the person who posted an item using the contact information they provided.
Resolve
Mark one of your posts as returned/resolved. Staff can moderate other users' posts.
Admin
Visible only to staff with access. Manage approvals, users, bans, sub-admins, and moderation.
Bottom-right Menu
Open Settings, this guide, Dark Mode, or Sign Out.
